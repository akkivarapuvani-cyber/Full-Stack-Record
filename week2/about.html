About the Training & Placement Department

Anil Neerukonda Institute of Technology & Sciences (ANITS) has a dedicated Training & Placement Department committed to providing students with excellent career opportunities through campus recruitment, industry collaborations, and skill development programs.

The department conducts comprehensive Campus Recruitment Training (CRT) covering Quantitative Aptitude, Logical Reasoning, Verbal Ability, English Communication, Group Discussions, and Interview Skills to prepare students for placement opportunities.

ANITS has consistently maintained an excellent placement record with leading multinational companies visiting the campus every year. Modern infrastructure, experienced trainers, and strong industry partnerships help students achieve successful careers.

Our Department Focuses On

  • Identifying employment opportunities for engineering graduates.
  • Building strong industry-academia partnerships.
  • Providing comprehensive campus recruitment training.
  • Organizing workshops, seminars and expert lectures.
  • Promoting innovation and entrepreneurship.
  • Facilitating internships and industrial visits.
